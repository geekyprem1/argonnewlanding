# ArgonMax bonus cover collection

Generated with the built-in Imagegen tool. Five separate images; bonus 01 was the shared visual reference for bonuses 02–05. Original PNGs are preserved in `source/`; optimized 960 × 640 WebP files are used by the landing page.

## Shared direction

Landscape 3:2 premium product mockup for an ArgonMax digital PDF bonus card on a dark navy AI software landing page. Polished realistic 3D hardcover PDF guide standing slightly angled facing front, slim page block visible on the right edge, on a low matte navy plinth. Deep midnight navy #071426 studio backdrop with a subtle luminous orbital ribbon. Soft blue rim lighting, realistic tactile materials, crisp edges. Book centered, entirely visible, with generous safe margins. Clean bold modern sans-serif typography, left-aligned, white with the key line in the accent color. No pricing, watermark, unrelated logos or extra books.

## Prompt set

All covers use top text `ARGONMAX`, pill `PDF GUIDE`, and bottom text `BONUS 01` through `BONUS 05` respectively.

1. **30-MINUTE / QUICK-START / GUIDE:** electric blue and cyan; abstract ascending architectural staircase motif in the lower half.
2. **300+ / PROMPT-TO-PROFIT / WORKFLOW VAULT:** warm amber and gold; intricate branching workflow pathways and connected nodes, embossed luminous lines.
3. **30-DAY CONTENT / & CAMPAIGN / CALENDAR:** emerald and turquoise; three-dimensional monthly calendar grid with glowing marked day squares.
4. **AI CLIENT-GETTER / AGENCY / STARTER KIT:** cobalt and soft violet; sculptural handshake formed from interlocking translucent glass shapes with connected network nodes.
5. **CREATIVE STUDIO / IMAGE & VIDEO / PROMPT PACK:** icy cyan and pale aqua; luminous camera aperture, cinematic clapperboard and floating image frames in frosted glass relief.

For companion covers, the first generated image was supplied as a style and composition reference. Instructions required matching its camera angle, book dimensions and placement, lighting, pedestal, materials, background, typography hierarchy and orbital ribbon, changing only the topic, number, symbol and accent treatment.
