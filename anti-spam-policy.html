Anti-Spam Policy

Our expectations for promotional communications.

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Permission-based communication

Promotional messages about ArgonMax should be sent only where the recipient has given appropriate permission or another lawful basis applies. Do not use purchased, scraped or harvested contact lists for unsolicited promotion.

Unsubscribing

Marketing messages should clearly identify the sender and provide a working way to unsubscribe. Use the unsubscribe option in the message you received. Essential purchase, security, access and support messages may still be needed to service your order.

Affiliates and partners

Promotional partners must follow applicable messaging laws and the rules of the platforms they use. Misleading sender information, deceptive subject lines and unsolicited bulk messages are not acceptable ways to promote ArgonMax.

Report unwanted messages

Report suspected spam through the ArgonMax Help Center. Include the sender, date, subject line, message headers if available and any referral link. Do not include passwords or payment-card details.

Handling reports

Reports can be reviewed to identify the sender and address misuse. Messages sent by independent third parties are not necessarily sent or authorized by ArgonMax. Contacting their email provider or the platform hosting the message may also help.
