Customer support

Visit the ArgonMax Help Center for available support channels and product guidance. Your purchase receipt may also list the seller's support contact details.

Purchase and account help

When contacting support, include your purchase email, order or transaction ID, purchase date and a short description of the issue. For a technical problem, mention your device and browser and describe the steps that led to it.

Refund requests

To use the advertised 30-day guarantee, contact support within 30 days of purchase and identify the order you want refunded. See the Refund Policy for details.

Privacy and promotional concerns

For privacy requests, unwanted messages or questions about an affiliate promotion, identify the relevant page, service or message so support can route your request.

Keep your details safe

Never send your account password, full payment-card number or authentication codes. Support may need information to verify your order, but your receipt or transaction ID is usually a useful starting point.
