Affiliate Compensation

How referral relationships can affect recommendations.

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Referrals to ArgonMax

Affiliates and promotional partners may receive a commission when someone purchases ArgonMax through their referral link. This relationship gives a partner a financial incentive to recommend the product.

Recommendations and reviews

A recommendation, review, bonus offer or testimonial from a promotional partner should be considered in light of that relationship. Partners are responsible for making clear disclosures near their promotional content. A partner's opinion does not guarantee your results.

Other products

If an ArgonMax promotion includes a compensated recommendation for another product or service, that relationship should be disclosed with the recommendation. Review the actual checkout price and seller terms before buying.

Independent purchase decision

Compare features and costs based on your own needs. An affiliate bonus or recommendation does not change the product features, guarantee or refund terms unless that benefit is expressly provided by the seller.

Questions

Use the ArgonMax Help Center to ask about an ArgonMax offer or report a misleading affiliate promotion. Include the page or referral link concerned.
