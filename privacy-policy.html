Privacy Policy

Information about this sales website and its connected services.

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Scope

This policy describes the ArgonMax sales website and purchase-support interactions. The product account, checkout, embedded-video and help-center services may also provide their own privacy notices. Review those notices before submitting information to those services.

Information you provide

When you purchase or request support, you may provide an email address, order details and the information in your support message. Payment details are submitted to the checkout and payment providers. Do not include passwords, full card details or sensitive personal data in a support request.

Website and device information

Hosting and connected providers may receive your IP address, browser and device details, page URL, referral information and access times when your browser requests content. This information can be used to deliver content, operate checkout, investigate errors and prevent abuse.

Embedded services and tracking

The sales page loads Launchpad checkout components and Wistia video content. The Launchpad component can load Google Tag Manager and send product-view information. These third parties may process device, interaction, cookie or referral information under their own policies. Links to external websites take you to services we do not operate.

Local storage and cookies

This site stores a countdown deadline in your browser local storage so the offer timer does not restart on refresh. Connected services may use their own cookies or storage. You can clear local storage and manage cookies in your browser; blocking them may affect some features.

Use and sharing

Information supplied for purchases or support is used to deliver access, identify orders, respond to requests, process refunds and protect the service. Service providers involved in those tasks may process that information. We do not request personal information for display in the public image or video galleries.

Retention, security and requests

Keep confidential information out of AI prompts unless the applicable product privacy terms support your intended use. For access, correction, deletion, retention or privacy questions, contact the ArgonMax Help Center with enough information to identify your request. Applicable rights depend on your location and the service processing the data. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Updates

We may update this policy as the website or its connected services change. The revision date appears at the top of this page.
